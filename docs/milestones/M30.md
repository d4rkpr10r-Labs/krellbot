# M30: paper P&L ledger and shared capital

**Status:** proposed; agents start M30 only after the owner merges it and
after earlier milestone files' agent tasks, unless the owner reorders.

**Depends on:** M28 (P01 live order truth: M28.3 and M28.22–M28.27, the F04
outcome and attribution tasks, including M28.26, the former PARKING line 20
fix). M28 books no paper skip codes (its F03 task, M28.8, is research-side
only), so M30.6 adds the `entry_skipped` field. M29 (M29.1, F22 run record,
and M29.5, job-kind validation, for the shared-capital research job).

**Exit / gate:** paper P&L reconciles to the paper balance per ownership
identity, and any mismatch is shown, never hidden. Paper fills export as a
CSV whose schema is documented. Before arming, the user sees committed versus
available capital, the order in which packs on one venue compete for cash and
the next entry's size against venue minimums. A shared-capital simulation
runs several packs against one Decimal ledger in declared pack order and
records every unfunded entry. A paper tick journals why an entry was skipped
for cash or minimums. No sizing rule, aggregate cap or ranking changes.

Rules for every task are in `AGENTS.md`. Agents skip `[owner]` tasks.
`PAPER_ONLY_RELEASE` stays True. Everything here is paper or backtest: label
it so and never present it as live P&L or market performance. Pinned shapes
stay as they are (`/paper/details`, the closed fleet row, the stored research
result, JobV1, the 'Owned position' sentence); new outputs go in new routes,
side-cars or new elements. UI follows `docs/design-system.md`
(`KRELL-SIGNAL-20261010`).

**Decisions adopted from the approved roadmap:**

- Decision 21: shared capital is allocated in declared pack order (today's
  config insertion order), and the UI says so. User ranking (F37) is left for
  later.
- Decision 18: PARKING line 18 stays. The shared-capital simulation takes
  distinct pairs only; nothing here puts two packs on one (venue, pair).
- The 'Owned position' sentence stays word for word. The owned versus
  external breakdown (salvaged from deferred F67) is a new element beside it.
- Exact per-fill fees come from one risk-path PR in `venues/paper.py`
  (M30.2), reviewed by the owner like any risk-path PR. Until it lands,
  fees are derived and labelled 'derived'.

**Parallelism:** M30.1 ∥ M30.4 ∥ M30.5 ∥ M30.7 first. M30.2 and M30.3 after
M30.1. M30.6 after M30.5. M30.8 after M30.7. M30.9 after M30.1.

## Epic M30-A — Paper P&L ledger and fills export

- [ ] **M30.1 Paper P&L ledger that reconciles to the paper balance.** A
  read-only projector in `application/paper.py` joins paper fills to journal
  `entry_coid` / `stop_fill_ids` per ownership identity (read-only import from
  `run/reconcile.py`): realized P&L (average cost), unrealized P&L, 'derived'
  fees, starting cash, owned versus external quantity, and a row reconciling
  to the actual paper balance that flags any mismatch. Unfilled requested
  quantity is never credited. Serve `GET /api/v1/paper/pnl-ledger`; render a
  new element beside the unchanged 'Owned position' sentence.
  - Scope: `src/krellbot/application/paper.py`, `src/krellbot/api/app.py`, `frontend/src/features/paper/PaperPnlLedger.tsx` (new), `frontend/src/features/paper/StatusPanel.tsx`
  - Deps: M28.3, M28.22–M28.27 (F04; M28.26 is the former PARKING line 20 fix)
  - Accept: `KRELLBOT_HOME=$(mktemp -d) uv run --no-sync pytest -q tests/test_paper_pnl_reconciles_with_balance.py && (cd frontend && npm run test:unit -- --run src/features/paper/PaperPnlLedger.test.tsx)`

- [ ] **M30.2 Record the fee and fee asset on every paper fill.** PaperVenue
  already deducts a fee (quantized ROUND_DOWN) but stores none on the fill, so
  the ledger re-derives it with different rounding. Add `fee` and `fee_asset`
  as additive keys on new paper fill records, equal to what was deducted from
  cash. Readers tolerate older fills without them. The M30.1 ledger uses the
  recorded fee when present and keeps 'derived' for older fills. No change to
  fill prices, sizing or stop behaviour.
  - Scope: `src/krellbot/venues/paper.py`, `src/krellbot/application/paper.py`
  - Deps: M30.1
  - Risk path: yes (`venues/paper.py`); label `risk-path`
  - Accept: `KRELLBOT_HOME=$(mktemp -d) uv run --no-sync pytest -q tests/test_paper_fill_records_deducted_fee.py`

- [ ] **M30.3 Paper fills CSV with a documented schema.**
  `POST /api/v1/paper/fills/export`, gated like `POST /api/v1/home/export`.
  One row per fill (venue, pair, base and quote asset, side, qty, price, fee,
  `fee_source` 'recorded' or 'derived_taker_bps', fee_asset, ts_ms, ISO-UTC
  time, fill id, order_coid, pack_id via the M30.1 join, mode=paper) plus a
  starting_cash seed row. Export-secret fields stay dropped; no tax figures.
  Schema in `docs/export-schema.md`; a `LastRunsPanel.tsx` export button
  reuses the `RehearsalFillsCsvExport` formula guard.
  - Scope: `src/krellbot/api/app.py`, `src/krellbot/application/paper.py`, `docs/export-schema.md` (new), `frontend/src/features/paper/LastRunsPanel.tsx`
  - Deps: M30.1
