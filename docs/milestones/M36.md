# M36: external signal intake

**Status:** proposed; agents start M36 only after the owner merges it and
after earlier milestone files' agent tasks, unless the owner reorders.

**Depends on:** M28 (P01: M28.3 and M28.22–M28.27, F04 live order truth, and
KQ-02 obligation rules, which any signal that later reaches the tick falls
under). M33 (M33.2 `exit_fraction`; until partial exits exist, partial
signals are refused). M36.1, the owner's relay decision, gates every agent
task here.

**Exit / gate:** previewed TradingView alerts are persisted, deduplicated by
fingerprint across restarts and checked for out-of-order arrival, in a store
whose failures can never hold entries. Each alert can be mapped to one
paper-armed revision and compared with the strategy's recorded position
(agrees, would enter, would exit, conflicts). No signal places an order or
enters the tick.

Rules for every task are in `AGENTS.md`. Agents skip `[owner]` tasks.
`PAPER_ONLY_RELEASE` stays True. The loopback boundary stays: no inbound
internet listener on the workstation, and the preview route keeps its
session, CSRF, origin and host checks. No `run/`, `execution/` or
`storage/outbox.py` edits. Reversal and partial requests are refused with
typed codes. UI follows `docs/design-system.md` (`KRELL-SIGNAL-20261010`).

**Decisions adopted from the approved roadmap:**

- Decision 12: hosted services (relay, AI, compute) are deferred until M28
  lands. TradingView cannot reach loopback, so this package needs a
  key-blind, outbound-only hosted relay (proposed ADR-0004); that choice
  stays with the owner (M36.1), and the local slices wait for it because
  without a relay they have little value.
- Receipts live in their own store file, never in `ops.sqlite`'s ledger
  table, which the outbox scans on every dispatch and the obligations reader
  loads on every tick.
- Any relay must be key-blind: exchange keys never leave the machine
  (PARKING line 12).

**Parallelism:** M36.2 after M36.1. M36.3 after M36.2.

## Explicitly out of M36

- Building or hosting the relay, and any outbound relay client (after
  M36.1, as their own tasks).
- Signals acting in the paper tick (risk-path `run/` under KQ-02 rules) and
  anything live.
- Reversal, short and partial signals.
