# M28: V1 live safety, operations health and honest disclosure

**Status:** proposed. Agents do not start M28 until the owner merges this
file. It books the Now (V1) packages P01–P04 of the approved demand roadmap.
M27's only open task is M27.5 [owner]. The research proposal (#625) is
renumbered M29 and waits behind M28 unless the owner reorders. No item is
booked in both files.

**Exit / gate:** on the shipped paper build, an alert notifies once per
occurrence, including `needs_reconcile` and store faults. Alerts reach the
operator from the scheduled tick without a workstation tab open. Every armed
feed shows its candle age and a classified failure code. Every place a user
decides states the fee, slippage, stop and limit assumptions the engine uses.
Settings says where keys and data live and what entitlements gate.

On fake transports with the release gate patched in-process
(`live_capable_release`), every live order outcome resolves from venue
evidence or an operator resolve. Strategy-owned quantity stays exact through
entries, stop fills and exits. A missing live stop is re-placed, not sold. A
rejected send records a refusal code. The kill switch keeps exits and stops
running, and a daily-loss gate and a close-position control exist. Kraken
private calls are spaced one second apart.
`PAPER_ONLY_RELEASE` stays True: M28 produces the evidence M27.5 and M20.6
require and flips nothing.

Rules for every task are in `AGENTS.md`. Agents skip `[owner]` tasks and keep
`PAPER_ONLY_RELEASE` True. Tests use fake transports, fake keyrings and an
isolated `KRELLBOT_HOME`, with no real orders, keys or new egress. The CLI
stays plumbing. Telemetry stays opt-in. Never present backtest, fixture or
paper results as market performance. UI follows `docs/design-system.md`
(`KRELL-SIGNAL-20261010`).

These shapes stay as they are:

- the 13-key receipt and the `receipt.metrics` keys;
- the stored `{legacy_receipt, trace}` result and `JobV1`;
- the `/api/v1/paper/details` body;

- the 'Owned position' sentence;
- LiveWizardPanel's 'Run krellbot doctor.' and 'Keys sent to krellbot.dev'

New outputs go in side-cars, new routes or new elements. A research side-car
is never a `*.json` file under `research-jobs/`, because `_restore_jobs`
parses every such file. A task edits an existing test only when it names that
test and the decision below that authorizes the edit. Tasks with a `Risk
path:` line carry the `risk-path` label.
