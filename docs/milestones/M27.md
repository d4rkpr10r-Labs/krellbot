# M27: spot exchange breadth and live qualification

**Exit:** Expand simulated spot coverage beyond the existing adapters and
produce the evidence needed to qualify a third live venue. The owner can
authorize a venue after reviewing qualification evidence. Release-wide
live enablement remains the separate M20 owner decision.

## Baseline

- Kraken and Coinbase are the two venues qualified for the live product path.
  The shipped release remains paper-only.
- The spot paper path on main also admits Binance, Gate, Pionex, OKX, Bybit,
  and KuCoin. Each addition has a pinned wave pair and fake-transport evidence.
  Passing one wave-pair test does not establish full exchange coverage.
- Ourbit (#600) and Toobit (#601) are the next paper spot additions. Include
  them in the same acceptance matrix and record their coverage separately.
- OctoBot advertises 15+ exchange integrations. Use that as a breadth target
  while tracking paper-ready spot, live-qualified spot, and supported pairs
  separately.

## Paper breadth

The first implementation in this proposal adds Binance spot public candles
to the scheduled paper tick. The older wave-pair tests used pinned CSV.
Other paper venues need the equivalent candle-reader and scheduler evidence.

- [x] **M27.1 Verify the paper venue acceptance matrix.** Exercise the same
  workstation flow per venue: create and validate a pack, arm its wave pair,
  seed instrument rules, tick with pinned data, and display the actual simulated
  balance and fill state. Cover stale rules, symbol mismatch, and an unsupported
  market. Verify the release refuses real orders without touching a transport.
- [ ] **M27.2 Add MEXC spot.** Start with `BTCUSDT` and the documented public
  exchange-info and market data interfaces. Verify precision, minimum order
  behavior, USDT funding, and workstation discovery with fixtures. Pin fees
  with a public source. Pull the venue into the shared allow-lists only when
  the paper arm and tick path are covered.
- [ ] **M27.3 Evaluate Hyperliquid spot and extend the candidate list.**
  Qualify spot symbol and token-pair mapping separately from perpetuals.
  Require a supported pair, public market data and instrument rules, and a
  release-fence test before shipping a connector. Use the same evidence to
  evaluate additional venues toward the 15+ target. Keep regional aliases
  under the exchange family unless they require distinct runtime semantics.

## Third live venue

- [ ] **M27.4 Add a fake-only Binance qualification path.** Use Binance as the
  first candidate beyond Kraken and Coinbase because its spot adapter and paper
  path already exist. Test trade-only key handling, refusal of withdrawal
  permission, venue-specific preflight, protective stop semantics, partial
  fills, deduplication, ambiguous submit reconciliation, and cancel/exit.
  Exercise sandbox responses with fixtures. Every write path must honor the
  existing release gate. Keep the candidate out of the qualified registry
  while acceptance and owner evidence are pending.
- [ ] **M27.5 [owner] Qualify the third venue.** Review the exact-head evidence,
  venue availability and policy, validate-only smoke, and paper soak. After
  approval, update the qualified registry, preflight, and workstation copy in
  the same PR. Preserve human live arm, minimize key scope, and keep
  `PAPER_ONLY_RELEASE` True until the separate M20 release decision.
