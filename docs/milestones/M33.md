# M33: exit sizing and capital release

**Status:** proposed; agents start M33 only after the owner merges it and
after earlier milestone files' agent tasks, unless the owner reorders.

**Depends on:** M28 (P01: M28.3 and M28.22–M28.27, F04 live order truth,
including M28.26, the former PARKING line 20 fix; M28.27, exit-residual
tracking; and M28.25, live stop repair through adapter `place_stop`). M30
(M30.1 paper P&L ledger, M30.7 shared-capital engine). M32 (M32.3 and M32.4
stop-mechanism and order-policy declarations).

**Exit / gate:** the workstation states how paper profits compound today and
how that differs from a reserved live arm. A pack can declare a partial exit
(`exit_fraction`); the backtest honours it with an explicit minimum-residual
rule, paper honours it with a re-protected residual, and every live arm path
refuses it. Default engine output stays byte-identical for packs without
the field.

Rules for every task are in `AGENTS.md`. Agents skip `[owner]` tasks.
`PAPER_ONLY_RELEASE` stays True. Decimal accounting, strategy-owned versus
external inventory and reconcile-never-resubmit hold in every task. Backtest
results with partial exits are labelled backtest, never market performance.
The 13-key receipt, `BarRecord` and the `test_eval_series.py` reference
output stay unchanged.

**Decisions adopted from the approved roadmap:**

- Decision 15: the additive `exit_fraction` pack-schema field is approved
  only together with its refusal on every arm path, in the same risk-path PR
  (M33.2): `PaperService.arm` and `run.arm_pack` both refuse it until residual
  re-protection exists. No other schema field rides in that PR.
- Decision 18: PARKING line 18 stays. A partial exit never creates a second
  deal on a pair; deal merging (F67) stays deferred.
- The F51 disclosure needs no owner decision. Live residual protection needs
  adapter `place_stop` on qualified venues (M28.25), so the live refusal stays
  in force through M33.

**Parallelism:** M33.1 ∥ M33.2 first. M33.3 after M33.2.

## Epic M33-B — Partial exits

- [ ] **M33.2 Add `exit_fraction` and refuse it on every arm path.** Optional
  additive `exit_fraction` in `pack/schema.json` and `pack/lint.py`, Target
  support in `pack/model.py` and `pack/evaluate.py`, and a partial sell in
  `backtest/engine.py` that sells everything when the residual would fall

## Explicitly out of M33

- Live partial exits (need adapter `place_stop` or resize on qualified venues
  after M28.25, and their own risk-path task).
- A configurable reinvest, release or withdrawal policy (a `run.tick` sizing
  change plus a persisted ArmedPack field; needs an owner-reviewed design).
- Several independently sized exit levels; dust previews.
- Deal merging and several deals per pair (F67 deferred, PARKING line 18).
